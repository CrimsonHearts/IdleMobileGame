  platform to improve stability.

## How we use information

- To provide and save your gameplay progress.
- To display advertising and offer in-app purchases.
- To diagnose crashes and improve the Game.

## Advertising & tracking

The Game uses third-party advertising (Google AdMob), which may use device
identifiers for personalized or non-personalized ads. On iOS you will be asked
for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency); you may decline.

You can reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings:
- Android: Settings → Google → Ads
- iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking

## Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your
region). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Your rights

Because gameplay data is stored locally, you can delete it at any time by
clearing the app's data or uninstalling the Game.
